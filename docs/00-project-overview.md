# 00 — Project Overview

**File:** `docs/00-project-overview.md`

## Project

**OKIKO HIGH Digital Marketing Agency** is building a new website to explain its digital marketing and software-as-a-service offerings, serve prospective clients worldwide, and generate qualified inquiries.

## Business description

OKIKO HIGH helps businesses strengthen their online presence, connect with customers, and build systems that support growth. Its approach combines digital marketing strategy with software and automation tools powered by GoHighLevel and its ecosystem.

**Mission:** Make marketing smarter, business operations easier, and growth more achievable.

## Project goals

The website should:

- Clearly explain Okiko High’s confirmed services and who they serve.
- Attract relevant worldwide search traffic and build topical authority.
- Provide useful answers for customers and search or AI answer systems.
- Turn qualified visitors into inquiries.
- Present the agency as collaborative, innovative, and focused on customer success.

Search and visibility goals include SEO, GEO, AEO, local-intent search where appropriate, LLM search, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Apple search presence, rich results, featured snippets, People Also Ask, knowledge panels, Perplexity, and ChatGPT. These are project objectives, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Initial audience focus

The planned primary audience groups are:

- **Home Services** — currently served.
- **Professional Services** — future opportunity.
- **Education** — future opportunity; exact subcategories TBD.
- **Healthcare and Wellness** — future opportunity.

Create a separate sub-audience page only when it can provide distinct, useful content. Avoid thin or duplicated pages.

## Service scope

Individual service pages are planned. On 2026-10-11 the owner confirmed availability for every service page built so far (see `docs/01`). Confirm availability before building a page for any other service, such as Google Business Profile automation or lead follow-up automation.

Candidate offerings include website design and development, AI chatbots, AI voice agents and receptionists, reputation management, Google Business Profile optimization and automation, SMS and email automation, and lead follow-up automation.

## Brand and design direction

- Use a **light-theme site** with a white foundation.
- Use colors sampled from the approved OKIKO HIGH logo. Exact color values are TBD.
- Do not use GoHighLevel logo assets or imply an official relationship.
- Use the approved flat OKIKO HIGH logo. The website-ready logo asset and file location are TBD.

## Technical direction

- **Framework:** Next.js
- **Source control:** GitHub
- **Deployment:** Cloudflare

Repository, domain configuration, and deployment settings will be documented as they are confirmed.

## Service area and geographic scope

The agency states that it serves clients worldwide. The site is not targeting Las Vegas, Nevada. Do not create city, state, or country landing pages by default. Add location-specific content only if a specific, substantiated strategy is established.

The Plano address, customer-facing status, hours, and Google Business Profile coverage require confirmation before they appear in public content or local business structured data.

## Project rules

- Use confirmed business information; mark unknown details **TBD**.
- Do not publish information belonging to **Okiko High Landscaping**.
- Give each page its own target keyword and unique metadata; use supporting keyword clusters naturally in headings.
- Treat structured data as a core implementation requirement and align it with visible page content and verified facts.
- Keep project documentation practical and lightweight. It should guide implementation without creating unnecessary approval gates.
